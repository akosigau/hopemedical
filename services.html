Compassionate, convenient, and personalized healthcare services for individuals and families in the comfort and privacy of their own homes, or by telehealth.

01

In-home medical care

We provide in-home & telehealth medical care for patients who may have difficulty traveling to appointments or prefer personalized care at home or place of residence.

  • Routine medical evaluations
  • Chronic disease management
  • Medication management
  • Follow-up care after hospitalization
  • Preventive healthcare services
  • Wellness assessments
  • Wound care
  • Coordination of care with specialists and caregivers
  • Coordination of diagnostic services including X-rays, ultrasounds, and laboratory testing when medically warranted

Questions

Good to know

How do I book an appointment?

Call 213-537-6956, fax 310-868-2763, or email eden@hopepromedgrpnc.com. You can also send a message through our contact page.

Do you offer both medical and mental health care?

Yes. We provide in-home & telehealth medical care, and compassionate mental health care with support and evaluations for anxiety, depression, stress-related conditions, mood disorders, behavioral concerns, and emotional wellness.

Can I see you at your office?

We're a mobile provider and don't see patients in an office. We come to you at home, or meet with you by secure telehealth.

Who is your care ideal for?

Seniors and adults, homebound patients, individuals with mobility challenges, and patients needing ongoing medical monitoring.

Which areas do you cover?

We offer telehealth across all of Northern California. Southern California residents can choose between in-person home visits and virtual care.
